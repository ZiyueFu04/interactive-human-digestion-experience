Core Concepts / 核心框架

Digestion is six linked jobs, not a list of organs.

The PDF begins with this system logic: the GI tract breaks food into absorbable forms, moves it, secretes fluids and enzymes, absorbs nutrients, and eliminates waste.
课件的起点是系统逻辑：胃肠道把食物分解为可吸收形式，推动、分泌、消化、吸收并排出废物。

Ingestion & Motility 摄入与运动

Taking food in, then using smooth muscle movement to transport it.

摄入食物，并依靠平滑肌运动完成运输。

Secretion 分泌

Water, acid, mucus, bicarbonate, bile and enzymes enter the lumen.

水、酸、黏液、碳酸氢盐、胆汁和酶进入管腔。

Mixing & Propulsion 混合与推进

Churning exposes food to secretions; peristalsis moves it aborally.

搅拌让食物接触分泌物；蠕动向远端推进。

Digestion 消化

Mechanical and chemical breakdown converts polymers into absorbable molecules.

机械和化学分解把大分子变成可吸收小分子。

Absorption 吸收

Most nutrients enter blood; fats enter lymph through lacteals.

多数营养进入血液；脂肪经乳糜管进入淋巴。

Defecation 排便

Water recovery, storage and reflex expulsion complete the journey.

水分回收、储存和反射性排出完成旅程。

Neural & Electrical Control / 神经与电活动控制

The gut has its own control layer.

Motility and secretion are not random. They are coordinated by ENS plexuses, autonomic input, slow waves, spike potentials and interstitial cells of Cajal.
胃肠运动和分泌不是随机发生，而由 ENS 神经丛、自主神经、慢波、动作电位和 Cajal 间质细胞协调。

Do Not Confuse / 不要混淆

Four mistakes that break the whole story.

These pairs are deliberately separated because they answer different physiological questions: what digests, what transports, what enters blood, and what triggers each hormone.
这些概念必须分开记：谁负责消化、谁负责运输、谁进入血液、什么刺激哪种激素。

Bile emulsifies fat but has no enzymes.

胆汁乳化脂肪，但没有消化酶。

Bile makes lipid droplets easier for pancreatic lipase to attack; the enzyme is pancreatic lipase, not bile.

Pepsin digests protein, not carbohydrate.

胃蛋白酶消化蛋白，不消化碳水。

Starch digestion starts in the mouth with salivary amylase and continues with pancreatic amylase.

Most nutrients enter blood; fats enter lymph.

多数营养入血，脂肪入淋巴。

Glucose and amino acids enter capillaries; chylomicrons leave enterocytes through lacteals.

Secretin responds to acid; CCK responds to fat/protein.

促胰液素响应酸，CCK 响应脂肪/蛋白。

Secretin drives bicarbonate-rich pancreatic juice; CCK drives bile release, pancreatic enzymes and satiety.
